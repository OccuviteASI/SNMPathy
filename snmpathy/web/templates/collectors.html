Collectors are SNMPathy installed at other sites. They connect out to this server (no inbound firewall rules at the site), pull the devices and checks assigned to them, poll and check them locally and send the results here - buffering them while the WAN is down. Devices behind a collector that stops reporting show as unreachable rather than down. Assign devices, checks and scans to a collector with their Monitored from / Run from setting.
